# Sea Monster Survival

An 8-bit browser game: pick a deep-sea cryptid (El Gran Maja, The Bloop, The Kraken, or Ningen),
choose your vessel and the time of day, then hunt it across a side-view ocean while it grows more
aggressive by the minute. Arm yourself with a harpoon, a scouting drone, and a pilotable mini-sub
that launches a steerable torpedo. Survive 20 minutes or kill it — before it swallows you whole.

Play: https://ryanhelsleypost.github.io/sea-monster-survival/

Controls: arrows / WASD / drag to move, space to fire, Q for drone, E to deploy the mini-sub.
